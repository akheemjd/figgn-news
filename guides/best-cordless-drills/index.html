Best Cordless Drills of 2026

Power Tools·Updated 2026-08-22

We tested the latest cordless drills from major brands to find the best models for different needs. From compact options for tight spaces to high-torque machines for heavy framing, these picks cover a range of budgets and skill levels.

What to look for

Before buying, consider the chuck size (1/2-inch is standard), battery voltage (18V-20V is common, 12V for lighter tasks), and whether you need a brushless motor for longer runtime and more power. Also check the clutch settings for driving screws without stripping, and the weight if you'll be using it overhead.
