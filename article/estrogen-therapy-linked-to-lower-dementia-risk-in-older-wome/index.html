Estrogen therapy linked to lower dementia risk in older women

Science & Health·ScienceDaily·2d ago

A large study of over 21,000 participants found that women who used estrogen-only hormone therapy later in life had a 39% lower odds of a dementia diagnosis and 35% fewer signs of Alzheimer's disease in their brains, according to ScienceDaily. The findings suggest a potential protective effect of estrogen therapy against dementia in older women. However, the study's observational nature means causality cannot be confirmed.
